This Privacy Policy explains how the “NMLS Exam Prep: SAFE MLO” mobile application (the “App”), operated by MD Shahariar Nazim Joy (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information.

None. The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or any personal information. We do not use third‑party analytics, advertising, or tracking software development kits (SDKs). The App does not request or access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, calendar, or health data.

Your study progress — including answered questions, scores, bookmarks, streaks, focus sessions, mock‑exam results, and onboarding preferences — is stored locally on your device only (using standard iOS or Android app storage). This information never leaves your device, is never sent to us, and is never shared with any third party. Deleting the App removes this information from your device.

In‑app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store using Apple’s StoreKit. We do not receive, process, or store your payment details or Apple Account information. Apple’s handling of your purchase information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. On Android, purchases are processed by Google through Google Play Billing, governed by Google’s Privacy Policy; we do not receive your payment details or Google account information.

4. Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell, rent, or share your data. Because the App does not collect personal data, we have no personal data to disclose.

5. Third‑Party Services

The only third‑party services used are Apple StoreKit (iOS) and Google Play Billing (Android), solely to offer and process in‑app purchases. The App makes no other network connections to us or to external servers.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for adults preparing for a professional mortgage‑licensing examination. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Security

Because your information remains on your device, it is protected by your device’s own security features (passcode, Face ID/Touch ID, and device encryption).
