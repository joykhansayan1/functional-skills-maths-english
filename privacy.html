Privacy Policy

Functional Skills Maths & English · Last updated: July 19, 2026

Summary: We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app runs on your device and works offline. No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

This Privacy Policy explains how the “Functional Skills Maths & English” mobile application (the “App”), operated by MD Shahariar Nazim Joy (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information.

1. Information We Collect

None. The App does not require an account or personal information. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The App does not request contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, calendar, or health data.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Study progress (answers, scores, bookmarks, streaks, mock results, onboarding preferences) is stored locally on your device only. It never leaves your device and is never shared. Deleting the App removes this data.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We do not receive or store your payment details. See Apple’s Privacy Policy. On Android, purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google through Google Play Billing; we do not receive your payment details or Google account information. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data. The App does not collect personal data.

5. Third-Party Services

Apple StoreKit (iOS) and Google Play Billing (Android) are used solely for in-app purchases. The App does not send study content to our servers.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for learners preparing for Functional Skills qualifications (typically age 16+ / adult education). It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Security

On-device data is protected by your device security (passcode, Face ID/Touch ID or Android screen lock, encryption).

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
